  PACKAGE DESIGN
  The objective for the project was to create a package to help promote any brands merchandise. The deliverables were to create three consistent packages. My client was the Life is good® brand which is most well known for its optimistic T-shirts and simple illustrations.

  My solution for this assignment was a packaged set of three car tire covers. Designing for an approachable brand, I kept the aesthetic friendly and vector graphics minimal that unifies the personality of the Life is good® company.
